# 📜 iRO Quests

The permanent quests of iRO (International Ragnarok Online) that Ragnarok Offline's server has no script for, written for Ragnarok Offline's rAthena 🗺️ The quest ids are the iRO client's own, so the quest window (**Alt+U**) shows iRO's own text for them.

It grows quest by quest. So far: **the Bounty Boards** 🎯, **Criminal Activities** 🕵️, **Eye of the Beholder** 👁️ and the **Overlook Water Dungeon** 🌊

## 🎯 Bounty Boards
iRO's repeatable hunting notices: **314 bounties on 65 boards** in 25 towns and 3 Illusion dungeons, by level range, usually next to the tool shop.

- 📌 **Take as many as you like.** Each asks for **150** of one monster; your quest log counts them, and kills by party members nearby, your homunculus or mercenary count too.
- 🏁 **Bring the finished hunt back to the board that posted it**, and it pays:
  - ✨ Base EXP = the monster's Base EXP × 150, Job EXP = its Job EXP × 150 (through the server's quest EXP rate, like any quest)
  - 💰 Zeny = the monster's level × 100
  - 🏅 every 4th bounty turned in: 1 **Eden Merit Badge**
- 🔁 Repeatable right away, no cooldown. Each board takes adventurers within its level range; a bounty you no longer want can be torn up at its board.
- 🟡 A board shows a yellow **?** when you have a finished bounty to turn in there.

### 🔍 Where it comes from
- **Boards**: their names, level ranges and positions are the iRO client's navigation table. The three Comodo boards stand one cell east of iRO's spot, which a Lasagna merchant already holds on this server.
- **Bounties**: the iRO client's quest table (62560-63748). iRO replaced most towns' boards with a newer set (63xxx); the older ones (62xxx) are kept only in Prontera, Geffen and Payon, which never got the new set, and for Juno's Venatus and Apocalypse, which iROWiki still lists there.
- **Rules and rewards**: iROWiki's *Bounty Board Quests* page.
- **Left out** (no board for them in the client, or no such monster on the server): Brasilis, the two Alliance camps, Archer Village, Artie's old Juno list, Bewler.

## 🕵️ Criminal Activities
iRO's crime caper for **Base Level 70+**. Agent York (Geffen inn) needs witnesses against Jymmy the Thumb, and Vince Noir's band mates are working for him against their will: buy, bribe or arm-wrestle Ling Mynmei's contract off Old Lady Kroot, flood Howard Moon's storage room through Geffen's four flood controls, then board the domestic airship to drug the guards, catch the rigged casino and fire the ship's cannon into its own engines.
- 🎁 1,000,000 Base EXP, 700,000 Job EXP and an Old Purple Box (iRO's non-VIP reward)
- 📍 Starts with **Agent York**, inside the Geffen inn (geffen_in 77, 57); ends in the Izlude armory

## 👁️ Eye of the Beholder
iRO's love story for **Base Level 50+**. Beho, a beholder in Rachel Temple, has fallen for Bianca, who is terrified of beholders. Get him White Chocolate from Kyara, a song from Doina, a necklace from Lilla (after finding her four bags of beads), a bouquet you pick for Kees, and the Pope's blessing.
- 🎁 500,000 Base EXP, 375,000 Job EXP, 5 Yggdrasil Berries and an Eden Merit Badge (iRO's non-VIP reward)
- 📍 Starts with the **Beholder** on the international airship's deck (or the one by Rachel Temple's entrance)

## 🌊 Overlook Water Dungeon
iRO's copy of the Byalan dungeon for **Base Level 30-85**, entered through **The Sign** west of Geffen (gef_fild07 264, 286). Its 15 **Aged** monsters (iRO's ids 2501-2515) have iRO's HP, DEF, MDEF, ATK, EXP and drops, drawn with their Byalan look. A Sign Board on each floor posts one hunt; clear all five and pick one Byalan card.
| Floor | Hunt | Base / Job EXP | Item |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 Aged Plankton, 20 Aged Marina | 60,660 / 68,040 | Guard [1] or Buckler [1] |
| 2 | 40 Aged Vadon, 40 Aged Kukre | 88,695 / 99,900 | Muffler [1] or Manteau [1] |
| 3 | 60 Aged Obeaune, 60 Aged Marse | 155,700 / 174,960 | 2 Elunium |
| 4 | 80 Aged Marc, 80 Aged Phen | 218,475 / 245,925 | Rosary |
| 5 | 100 Aged Strouf, 100 Aged Merman | 335,340 / 377,190 | Rosary, 2 Elunium |

On iRO it is a VIP dungeon; this server has no VIP, so it is open to everyone in the level range.

## ⚙️ Settings
In **Settings → Mods → iro-quests**:
- **Bounty Boards**, **Criminal Activities**, **Eye of the Beholder**, **Overlook Water Dungeon**: one switch each, all on by default. Turning a quest off removes its NPCs; quest logs and progress stay, except for the Overlook hunts, which leave with their monsters.

## 💾 Installing
Renewal servers, Ragnarok Offline 1.6.7 or newer. Download `iro-quests-v<version>.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page and install it with **Settings → Mods → Install from a folder…**, then restart the server.

## 🚀 Releases
- ✅ Every pull request runs [validate.yml](.github/workflows/validate.yml): `mod.json`, every `.yml` with rapidyaml, the CHANGELOG, the release zip's layout, and the scripts on an rAthena map-server `--run-once`.
- 🏷️ Publishing a GitHub Release tagged `v<version>` builds `iro-quests-v<version>.zip` ([release.yml](.github/workflows/release.yml)) and attaches it. What changed is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- 🛠️ The Bounty Boards, the quest table and the Aged monsters are generated by [tools/bounty](tools/bounty) and [tools/overlook](tools/overlook) from the client's tables and the iRO database; the story quests are hand-written from iROWiki's walkthroughs; [triage/RECHECK.md](triage/RECHECK.md) is the checked list of iRO quests still to do.
