# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- New: **Gramps' weekly turn-ins** (iRO, Base Level 70-150) at the Eden Group: 400-kill hunts, two monsters a week in each of three tiers (184 hunts from the iRO client), paying 400x the monster's EXP, 65,000 zeny and an Eden Merit Badge, with a 16-hour Hunting Timer. Replaces rAthena's talk-only Gramps.
- New: **City Investment** (kRO/iRO City Merchant Union): join at the Eden Group (Union Manager, Bulletin Board, Secretary Governor), get Union Insignias from monsters (about 1 in 5) and from requests (111 A/B/C deliveries to NPCs in dungeons, 20-50 Insignias; 23 weekly Wanted bosses, 100-300), invest up to 250 a day in one of 9 cities and buy its account-bound Union gear as your standing grows.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, Morroc: the Elderly Scholar, his apprentice, Lumin, Ernest and Leader Maxim's pyramid team (13 story steps), then their dailies, shared with the Eden Group's mission boards.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, Payon: Durevak's liquor, the lost grandson and Reno in Payon Cave, the elder's tale and the Moonlight Flower fox hunt, Dong Dong's firewood, the worried neighbours' boa, "onion" and mushroom hunts, the Wandering Monk's ghost fires and the Young Apprentice's handkerchiefs, with 9 dailies.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, Prontera: Paul and Ann's playground hunts outside the west gate; the Knights' Orc and Goblin subjugation (supply run from the Knights' office to Orc Village, 6 hunts); Priest Leader Lars, Claude and the Glast Heim monastery (Tatiana and Marylin pray for you), Charon and Nadir at the castle. 13 dailies.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, Izlude: Instructor Pontes' training requests, Student Helden and the Wandering Store Owner on Byalan Island, and Leader Maxim's research team in the undersea tunnel (Ernest, Kusekili, Ankana). 9 dailies.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, Aldebaran and the Juno fields: the homemade lunch for the checkpoint guard, Commander Cesare, the Kiel Hyre Academy's student, guard and Cat Paw Merchant, and the Clock Tower Manager's four clock tower hunts. Hugel: Fisherman Ein and Blacksmith Dry. Einbroch, Einbech and Lighthalzen: Ernst's letter and Friedrich's commuter pass to Einbech, Guard Artin's mine, Guard Eisen, Theodore and Reno. 23 dailies.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, Geffen: the Apprentice Mage's tower-dungeon hunts, the Tired Man's Nightmares and the Petit Boy's Petites. Rachel and Veins: the airport guard, Leonard and Craven. Comodo: Que Sera, Sera (Chef Bobby Q, Serabi, Bunny Q), the Vigilantes and the Explorer. Alberta: Kale and Timothy, Mulberry's shipwreck treasure, Aspar and Drake's Ghost; Knight Tybalt and the Knight Commander on Turtle Island. 26 dailies.
- New: **Town quests (2021 revamp)**, the field givers of the Eden Group's missions: Leader Maxim's team at Juperos, Nahadan at Abyss Lake (with the injured traveler), Morvand (Odin Temple), the campfire traces (Bio Lab), the Museum Staff (Magma Dungeon), Avril and Tart (Thanatos Tower), Darkess and Chiab (Glast Heim), Yerne, Theodore and Niu Niu (the other world), Lartal, Rotert (Nameless Island) and Rachel and Veins' farmers, hunters and tavern keeper. 76 dailies, the same missions as the boards.
- Fix: the town quests' daily hunts and cooldowns are now defined in this mod too (word for word as in eden-rework-2021), so they work with that mod switched off.

## 0.1.0
- First part: iRO's Bounty Boards. 314 repeatable 150-kill hunts on 65 boards in 25 towns and the Moonlight, Vampire and Frozen Illusion dungeons, each with iRO's level range and position. Rewards: the monster's EXP x 150, its level x 100 in zeny, and an Eden Merit Badge every 4th bounty.
