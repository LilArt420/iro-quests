# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- New: **Gramps' weekly turn-ins** (iRO, Base Level 70-150) at the Eden Group: 400-kill hunts, two monsters a week in each of three tiers (184 hunts from the iRO client), paying 400x the monster's EXP, 65,000 zeny and an Eden Merit Badge, with a 16-hour Hunting Timer. Replaces rAthena's talk-only Gramps.

## 0.2.0
- New: **Criminal Activities** (iRO, Base Level 70): Agent York, Vince Noir's band and Jymmy the Thumb's casino airship, from Geffen to Izlude. 1,000,000 / 700,000 EXP and an Old Purple Box.
- New: **Eye of the Beholder** (iRO, Base Level 50): Beho and Bianca in Rachel. 500,000 / 375,000 EXP, 5 Yggdrasil Berries and an Eden Merit Badge.
- New: **Overlook Water Dungeon** (iRO, Base Level 30-85): The Sign west of Geffen, the 15 Aged monsters and the 5 floor hunts with their card reward.
- Bounty Boards: every board menu now says what it's asking, on a clean window.

## 0.1.0
- First part: iRO's Bounty Boards. 314 repeatable 150-kill hunts on 65 boards in 25 towns and the Moonlight, Vampire and Frozen Illusion dungeons, each with iRO's level range and position. Rewards: the monster's EXP x 150, its level x 100 in zeny, and an Eden Merit Badge every 4th bounty.
